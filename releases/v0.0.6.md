# v0.0.6 - A smoother reader, smarter page caching, and download recovery

**Released 2026-10-21**

Mangora 0.0.6 improves the reading experience, reduces repeat image downloads,
updates on-device text recognition, and makes it easier to restore a download
library if the app's local index is lost.

## What's new

- **Faster chapter revisits.** Reader images are kept in a shared cache used by
  both the page viewer and translation. Reopening a recently viewed chapter
  can avoid fetching its images again. The cache keeps up to two chapters and
  removes the least recently used chapter when space is needed.
- **Downloaded chapters work with the shared reader cache.** When you open a
  downloaded chapter, its pages are extracted temporarily from the `.cbz` into
  the reader cache. The archive remains in your selected downloads folder.
- **Choose whether reader images are cleared at startup.** Settings > Data &
  Storage now includes **Clear reader cache on app start**. It is on by
  default, and clears only temporary reader images on a cold app start;
  downloaded chapters, translations, and OCR models are kept.
- **Recover your download library.** Settings > Downloads can rebuild the
  app's download list from `downloads/metadata/metadata.json`. Mangora checks
  each listed archive before restoring it and shows progress and any chapters
  it could not recover. Recovery needs the metadata file; without it, Mangora
  will not guess chapter identities from filenames.
- **Safer download checks and cleanup.** Completed archives are checked for
  valid page images before they are listed as downloaded. Bulk recovery and
  deletion show progress; deletion leaves files that cannot be identified by
  the catalog untouched.
- **More flexible reading controls.** Reader settings make it easier to
  choose a reading layout, adjust page-turn tap zones, and tune image
  presentation with options such as split wide pages, border cropping,
  rotation, and display filters.
- **Updated on-device OCR models.** PP-OCRv6 is available for Japanese,
  Chinese, and English; PP-OCRv5 covers Korean and Latin-script languages.
  Recognition uses text-line detection and batching for clearer processing.
- **More resilient AI translation.** With Auto model switch enabled, Gemini
  translation can retry a rate-limited request using the other available
  model. The setting can be turned off if you prefer to keep requests on the
  selected model.

## Good to know

- Reader images are temporary copies and are separate from downloaded `.cbz`
  chapters. The startup-clear setting is enabled by default, so cached images
  are not retained across a cold app restart unless you turn it off.
- Recover downloads relies on the `metadata.json` file in the selected
  downloads folder. Keep that file with the chapter archives if you move or
  back up the folder.
- Translation still depends on the OCR model selected for the page language.
  Download the relevant model in Settings > OCR models before translating.
- Mangora remains Android-only. Online translation sends recognized text to
  the selected translation service; OCR itself runs on-device.

## Install

ARM64 Android 7.0+ only - 32-bit ARM is not supported.
