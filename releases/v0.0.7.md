# v0.0.7 - Simpler reader modes, better zoom, and smoother navigation

**Released 2026-10-28**

Mangora 0.0.7 makes the reader easier to set up and more comfortable to use,
keeps your preferred manga view between visits, and fixes translated text on
devices that use a right-to-left language.

## What's new

- **Three clear ways to read.** The reader now offers Paged, Long strip with
  gaps, and Long strip. Paged shows one page at a time; the two long-strip
  options let you scroll continuously, with or without space between pages.
- **Better zoom in long strip.** Pinch to zoom into long-strip chapters and
  move around the page naturally. Zoom now stays stable while you keep reading.
- **Better zoom on paged manga.** Paged manga has smoother, more reliable zoom,
  making it easier to inspect artwork and small details.
- **Your manga view is remembered.** Choose two columns, four columns, or a
  list in Settings > Display, and extension sources will keep using that layout
  on your next visit.
- **Translations stay in place on right-to-left devices.** Translated text now
  stays aligned with the original speech bubbles on phones using Arabic or
  another right-to-left language. Manga page direction is still controlled
  separately in the reader.
- **Smoother movement around the app.** Menus, panels, tabs, and loading
  screens now use gentle transitions instead of appearing abruptly.

## Good to know

- Reader settings are saved automatically and apply to the next chapter you
  open.
- Right-to-left paging remains available in the reader when a manga needs it;
  it is independent of your phone's language.

## Install

Android 7.0+ (API 24+). Choose the APK that matches your device architecture.
