## Planned

- **PC app.** Build a desktop version of Mangora for reading and translating
  manga on a PC. Platform support, packaging, and feature parity will be
  decided as the desktop design develops.
- **Local PC-to-Android translation bridge.** Let the PC app act as a
  translation server on the local network so the Android app can send it
  translation requests. Pairing, access control, and what data crosses the
  connection will be specified before implementation.
- **App guides with screenshots.** Publish practical, step-by-step guides for
  setup, extensions, reading, downloads, and translation, with screenshots of
  the released Android app beside the relevant steps.
